Do the new triatomine species pose new challenges?

Abstract Chagas disease persists as one of the most important neglected diseases.

Key words:
American Trypanosomiasis; vectors; Triatominae

Despite the great achievements in controlling Chagas disease, also known as American Trypanosomiasis, major problems are still to be solved in Latin American countries.2

The most effective action is the elimination of Triatoma infestans and Rhodnius prolixus.7

Carlos Chagas described the disease when transmission was due to T. infestans.
